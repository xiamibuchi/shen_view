# ShenView: App store listing

Draft listing. Use only after the corresponding release build passes functional acceptance. The app is not listed in stores yet.

### App name

ShenView

### Subtitle

Local photos and comic reading

### Short description

Browse local pictures and comics with a thumbnail grid, Double page and Continuous reading modes, animation controls and HDR viewing on compatible setups.

### Full description

**Your pictures, in focus.**

ShenView is a desktop image viewer built around local files and folders. Open a photo, explore a folder or start reading a comic inside an archive.

**Explore your folders**

Switch folders in the directory panel, find pictures in the thumbnail grid and move through nearby images with the filmstrip. Recursive folder browsing and natural sorting keep image sequences easy to follow.

**Choose your view**

Switch between Single page, Double page, Long image and Continuous reading modes. Fit pictures to the window or width, or view them at actual size. Use Auto play to change images at a set interval or scroll automatically.

**Read inside archives**

Browse images in ZIP, CBZ, RAR and CBR archives without manually extracting them first. Use directory navigation and comic reading modes to pick up where you left off.

**Take control of animations**

Pause, step through frames, seek to a moment and adjust playback speed to see the details.

**Look a little closer**

Zoom in, pick colors and inspect image information. View a range of common and extended image formats, with HDR viewing for compatible images, decoding paths and displays. SDR fallback keeps pictures viewable when HDR is unavailable.

**Make room for your pictures**

Choose a light or dark theme, then use fullscreen or borderless viewing to suit your space.
